What is Forward Deployed Engineering?

FDE means we don't advise from a distance. We sit in your Slack, your codebase and your stand-ups. We find where a model genuinely beats the status quo, and we say so, loudly, when it doesn't.

Then we build. Agents that do work, not demos. Retrieval (RAG) over your actual documents. Copilots for the people doing the job. Automation wired into your CRM, ERP, mail and docs. Everything ships with evals, guardrails, observability and a human in the loop, because a model without those is a liability with a chat window.

When it works, we hand over documented code and train your team to run it. Then we go. That's the whole point.

  • Engineers first. Nobody on the team sells what they can't build.
  • Working software in weeks, on your real data, in your real systems.
  • Honest: we'll tell you if AI is the wrong tool. Sometimes it's a cron job.
  • Handover, not lock-in. GDPR-safe by architecture, not by footnote.

// Mission phases · typical arc

Eight weeks. Then you own it.

00
Week 0

Embed

We get access and sit with your team. We learn the actual workflow, not the org-chart version of it. Who touches what, where the time goes, what's already been tried.

Abort condition: none yet
01
Week 1–2

Prototype

A working prototype on your real data, in front of the people who'd use it. Not a mockup, not a deck. We measure it against the current way.

Abort condition: it doesn't pay off. We stop and say so.
02
Week 3–8

Harden

Evals, permissions, monitoring, guardrails, fallbacks. Integrations into the systems you actually run. We load it, break it, fix it, and write it down.

Abort condition: it can't be made safe. Rare, but we've seen it.
03
Week 8+

Hand over

Documented code in your repo, a trained team, and us on call. You own the system. If you want us back, that should be a choice, not a dependency.

Exit condition: your team ships the next change without us.
