services

Four things we do

01Mapping

02Build & integrate

agents, retrieval over your documents, copilots for your team, automations. wired into your CRM, ERP, mail and docs. shipped with evals, permissions, monitoring, and a person who can say no to the machine.

documented code, a handover your team can actually run, and training so you don't need us afterwards. bad business model, good for you.

how an engagement goes

From embed to handover

  1. week 0
    Embed

    we join your channels and standups, get access sorted, and start asking annoying questions.

  2. week 1–2
    Prototype

    a working thing on your real data. ugly, but true. you'll know by now if it's worth continuing.

  3. week 3–8
    Harden

    evals, permissions, monitoring, edge cases. the stuff that makes it survive a Monday morning.

  4. after
    Hand over

    docs, training, keys. we stay on call, but the goal is that you rarely call.
