What is Forward Deployed Engineering?

A forward deployed engineer is not a consultant who writes a report and disappears. An FDE moves into your company, sits with the people doing the work, and builds the thing with them, on your real data, inside your real systems.

For AI this matters more than usual. The demos are easy. The hard part is the boring part: which of your fifty workflows actually gets better with a model in the loop, how the agent gets permission to touch your CRM without wrecking it, what happens when it is wrong, and who gets paged. That is engineering work, and it only gets done well from the inside.

So that is what we sell. We embed, we prototype on your data in the first two weeks, we harden it with evals, guardrails, observability and a human in the loop, and then we hand over the code, documented, and train your team to own it. If we discover that AI is the wrong tool for your problem, we will say so before you have paid for the wrong thing.

How an engagement plays

Four levels. Every one ends with something running, not a document about something that might run.

  1. World 1-1

    Embed

    Week 0

    We get badges, accounts and a desk near the people who do the work. We read your systems, not your strategy deck.

  2. World 1-2

    Prototype

    Week 1–2

    A working version on your real data, used by real colleagues. Ugly is fine. Fake is not.

  3. World 1-3 · Boss stage

    Harden

    Week 3–8

    Evals against real cases, permissions and guardrails, monitoring, fallbacks, a human in the loop where it counts. This is where most AI projects die. Ours do not.

  4. World 1-4 · Castle

    Hand over

    Then onwards

    Code, docs and training go to your team. We stay on call. The princess is in this castle.
