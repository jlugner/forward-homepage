Our approach

What Forward Deployed Engineering means in practice

Most AI initiatives stall between a promising demonstration and a system people rely on. The gap is rarely the model. It is integration, data access, permissions, evaluation and ownership. Forward Deployed Engineering closes that gap by placing the people who build the system inside the organisation that will run it.

  • Engineers firstEvery engagement is staffed by senior engineers who write and ship the code. There is no separate advisory layer between you and the people doing the work.
  • Real workflows, real dataWe start from the tasks your staff carry out today and build against your systems: CRM, ERP, email, document stores and internal databases.
  • An honest assessmentIf a language model is the wrong tool for a given problem, we will say so in the first two weeks and recommend an alternative, including conventional software or no change at all.
  • Handover, not lock-inYou own the source code, the evaluation data and the documentation. Your team is trained to operate and extend the system without us.

Services

Four services, one engineering team

Engagements typically move through the first, second and fourth service in sequence. Private inference is offered where regulatory or contractual requirements prevent the use of external model providers.

Opportunity mapping

A structured, two-week review of where AI will and will not pay off in your operations, grounded in observed work rather than interviews alone.

  • Shadowing and process analysis across selected functions
  • Data access and quality assessment
  • Prioritised backlog with effort, risk and expected value
  • Clear recommendation, including where not to proceed

Build and integrate

Production systems built inside your environment: agents, retrieval over your documents, copilots for specific roles, and automation across your line-of-business systems.

  • Integration with CRM, ERP, email and document platforms
  • Evaluation suites that run before every change
  • Guardrails, permission scoping and audit logging
  • Observability and human-in-the-loop review by design
In development

Private and on-premises inference

For organisations whose data cannot leave their control, we deploy and operate open-weight models within your own infrastructure or in an EU-resident environment.

  • Model selection and benchmarking against your tasks
  • Deployment on your hardware or dedicated EU capacity
  • No data transferred to third-party model providers
  • Capacity planning and cost modelling

Enablement and handover

Your team learns to operate, evaluate and extend the systems we build together, so that the capability stays with you after the engagement ends.

  • Pairing with your engineers throughout the build
  • Runbooks, architecture documentation and decision records
  • Workshops for engineering, product and operations staff
  • Optional retained support after handover

Process

From first day to handover in about eight weeks

The engagement is structured in four stages with a defined outcome at each. Commercial decision points fall at the end of stages one and two.

  1. 1Week 0

    Embed

    Two engineers join your team on site or remotely. We agree scope, obtain system access and observe the target workflows as they are performed today.

    Outcome: agreed scope, access in place, and a shortlist of candidate workflows.

  2. 2Weeks 1–2

    Prototype on real data

    A working prototype connected to your actual systems and documents, tested by the staff who would use it. This is where we confirm, or rule out, that the approach is viable.

    Outcome: a functioning prototype and a go or no-go recommendation.

  3. 3Weeks 3–8

    Harden

    Evaluation suites, permission scoping, monitoring, logging and human review paths. The system is integrated into your deployment and security processes.

    Outcome: a production system with measured quality and an operating runbook.

  4. 4After

    Hand over, stay on call

    Source code, documentation and evaluation data are transferred to your organisation. Your team is trained to operate and extend the system. We remain available under a support agreement if required.

    Outcome: full ownership, with optional retained support.

Security and compliance

Built to pass your security review, not to work around it

We work within your existing controls. Data handling, access and logging are agreed in writing before any production data is processed.

GDPR

GDPR by design

Data processing agreements, data minimisation and documented legal bases. We support your DPIA where one is required.

EU residency

Data residency

Processing and storage within the EU, or within your own infrastructure, as your policy requires. No undisclosed sub-processors.

On-prem

On-premises inference

Where external model providers are not acceptable, models run on your hardware or on dedicated capacity under your control.

Audit

Audit logging

Every model input, output, tool call and human decision is logged in a form your internal audit and compliance functions can query.

Permissions follow the user. Systems we build retrieve only what the requesting user is already authorised to see. Access control is inherited from your identity provider, not re-implemented.

People stay in the loop. Actions with financial, legal or customer impact require human approval by default. Automation thresholds are raised only as measured accuracy supports it.

Frequently asked questions

Commercial and practical questions

Opportunity mapping is a fixed-price engagement of two weeks. Build and hardening are priced per engineer per week, with a defined team size agreed in advance, typically two engineers. Retained support after handover is offered as a monthly agreement with a defined response time. We do not charge licence fees for the software we build; it is yours.

A first workflow typically reaches production in six to eight weeks from the day we join. Two weeks of that are spent on the prototype, after which you decide whether to proceed. Timelines depend mainly on how quickly system access can be granted and on the availability of the staff whose work we are supporting.

You do. All code, prompts, evaluation datasets, documentation and configuration produced during the engagement are assigned to your organisation on payment. Forward retains the right to reuse general techniques and internal tooling that contain none of your data or business logic. This is set out in the master services agreement.

Your team operates the system using the runbook and evaluation suite delivered with it. Many clients choose a retained support agreement for the first three to six months, covering incident response, model upgrades and re-evaluation as providers change their models. Others proceed independently. Either is a normal outcome.

We are provider-independent and select models based on measured performance on your tasks, cost and your data-handling requirements. Where external providers are used, we contract for EU processing and no training on your data. Where that is insufficient, we deploy open-weight models within your infrastructure. The choice is documented and reviewable.

We will tell you, in writing, at the end of the prototype stage at the latest. The engagement then ends at the agreed decision point, and you retain the analysis and any code produced. We would rather end an engagement early than deliver a system that does not justify its cost.
