01Briefing

Forward deployed means embedded.

Every corp has the same demo: a chatbot that impressed the board and never touched the floor. The gap between that demo and something your people use every day is filled with integrations, permissions, edge cases and trust. That gap is our turf.

We jack into your organisation. We sit with the people doing the work. We build AI tooling around how things actually run, wire it into the systems you already have, and make it observable, evaluated and safe. When we leave, your team owns every line.
