What forward deployed means

We sit next to the problem, not in a meeting about it.

The gap between an impressive AI demo and something your team uses every day is enormous. It is filled with integrations, permissions, edge cases and trust. That gap is where we work.

We come into your organisation, learn the real workflows, and build tooling around them. When we leave, your people own the code and understand it.

Services

Discover. Build. Run.

01

Opportunity mapping

Two days on the floor with your teams. We map the workflows, rank them by leverage, and hand you a costed roadmap. Sometimes the answer is "not AI". We will say so.

02

Build & integrate

Agents, retrieval, copilots and automations wired into the systems you already run. Evals and observability included, always.

03

Private inference

Data that cannot leave the building stays in the building. On-prem or private cloud, open models.

Team enablement

Process
