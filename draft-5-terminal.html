name

forward - forward deployed engineering for ai

synopsis

forward [--embed] [--ship] [--handover] <your-company>

description

three senior engineers from sweden. we sit inside your company, find the workflows where ai actually pays off, and build them into production. agents, retrieval, copilots, automation, wired into the crm, erp, mail and docs you already have. with evals, guardrails, observability and a human in the loop.

then we hand over the code, documented, train your team, and get out of the way.

"forward deployed" means the engineer is on your side of the firewall. no account manager between you and the person who writes the code.

options

--no-slides
default. cannot be disabled.
--weeks
the unit of time we ship in. months not supported.
--honest
we will tell you if ai is the wrong tool. exits 1 and saves you money.
--handover
you own the code, the docs and the keys. always set.
--lock-in
unknown option.
--gdpr
always on. data stays in the eu, or on your own metal.

guest@forward:~$ services

services

four things we do. one thing we don't.

mapping
two weeks inside your company. we shadow the work, read the tickets, sit in the meetings. output: a short list of where ai pays off, and a longer list of where it doesn't.
build
agents, retrieval/rag, copilots, automation. integrated with crm, erp, mail, docs. evals from day one, permissions, monitoring, human-in-the-loop. production, not demo.
private-inference
models running in your vpc or on your own hardware, for the data that must not leave the building. later: our own boxes. ask.
enablement
your team learns to own what we built. runbooks, pairing, the boring parts. handover, not retainer.
slide-decks
not found.

process

the engagement arc. every client, same shape.

week 0embedbadge, laptop, slack. we sit with the people who do the work, not the people who talk about it. week 1-2prototypeon your real data, in your real systems. ugly and working beats pretty and fake. week 3-8hardenevals, permissions, monitoring, failure modes, and the human who has to approve things. week 8+handoverdocs, training, keys. you own it. we stay on call.
