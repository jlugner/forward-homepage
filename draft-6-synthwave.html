What is Forward Deployed Engineering?

The people who build the system sit where the problem is. That's the whole idea. Everything else follows from it.

Side A · What it means

We join your team for a fixed engagement, learn how the work actually gets done, and build AI into the workflows where it pays off, not where it looks best in a demo.

We write production code: agents, retrieval over your documents, copilots for your teams, automation, and the integrations with the CRM, ERP, mail and document systems you already run. Every build ships with evals, guardrails, observability and a human in the loop where it matters.

Side B · What it isn't

Not a strategy deck. Not a proof of concept that lives in a notebook forever. Not a black box you rent from us by the month.

If AI is the wrong tool for the job, we say so in week one, not month six. And when it's done, the code is yours: documented, handed over, with your team trained to run it. Your data stays in the EU, under your permissions, and we treat GDPR as a design constraint rather than a footnote.

Track listing

The arc of an engagement

Fixed scope, real data from the first week, and an ending written in from the start.

A1
Week 0

Embed

We sit with your team, get access, read the systems, and pick the first workflow together. No discovery phase that outlives its welcome.

A2
Week 1–2

Prototype on real data

A working version on your actual data, in front of the people who will use it. Not a slide about one. This is where we find out if it's worth hardening.

B1
Week 3–8

Harden

Evals, permissions, monitoring, guardrails, human-in-the-loop. The unglamorous parts that turn a demo into something you can trust on a Tuesday.

B2
After

Hand over, stay on call

Documented code in your repo, a trained team, and a phone number that still answers. You own it. We're around if you want us.
