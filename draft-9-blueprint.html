1ScopeZone A

  1. 1.1

    This specification covers the supply of forward deployed engineering (FDE) for applied AI by Forward AB ("Consultant") to the purchasing organisation ("Client").

  2. 1.2

    "Forward deployed" shall mean that the Consultant works inside the Client's organisation, with the Client's data, systems and people. Not from a remote office. Not from a slide.

  3. 1.3

    Scope includes: agents, retrieval (RAG), copilots, workflow automation, and integrations with CRM, ERP, mail and document systems. Evaluation, guardrails, observability and human-in-the-loop controls are included by default and shall not be sold as options.

  4. 1.4

    Scope excludes: strategy decks, AI roadmaps not attached to a build, and any system the Consultant would be embarrassed to see running in production.

  5. 1.5

    All deliverables shall run on the Client's infrastructure, under the Client's accounts, prior to acceptance. Screenshots do not constitute delivery.

  6. 1.6

    Where a workflow can be improved without AI, the Consultant shall say so, in writing, and shall propose the simpler tool. Spreadsheets are acceptable tools.

Definitions

Working software
A system that runs on real data, for real users, and can be turned off by the Client.
Week
Five working days. Swedish public holidays excluded. Estimates given in quarters are not weeks.
Handover
The moment the Client's engineers can operate, extend and delete the system without calling us.
Slide deck
See 4.3.

2Services · Details A–DZone B

Detail A · FWD-D-A · Scale 1:1

Mapping

2.1.1Survey of existing workflows, as-built. Consultant shall interview the operators, read the tickets, and measure the process as it is actually performed, not as documented.

2.1.2Output: a ranked list of three to five candidate workflows with estimated payoff, risk, and data readiness, each with a proposed first build.

2.1.3Where no candidate exceeds the payoff threshold, the list shall say so. "None" is a valid finding and is invoiced at the same rate.

Duration 1–2 wkCrew 1–2 engineersDeliverable ranked list, first build spec
Detail B · FWD-D-B · Scale 1:1

Build & integrate

2.2.1Production implementation of the selected candidate(s). Includes retrieval over Client data, agent tooling, model routing, and integrations with CRM, ERP, mail and document systems.

2.2.2Includes an evaluation suite, a permission model matching the Client's existing roles, audit logging, monitoring, fallbacks, and human review points at every irreversible action.

2.2.3Prototype on real data by end of week 2. Hardened by end of week 8. Model vendor shall be swappable; Consultant shall demonstrate the swap.

Duration 3–8 wkCrew 2–3 engineersDeliverable running system, repo, evals
Detail C · FWD-D-C · Scale TBD

Private / on-prem inference

2.3.1For Clients whose data shall not leave the building. Consultant shall size, install and operate local inference hardware with open-weight models on Client premises or in a Swedish datacentre.

2.3.2No prompts, documents or logs shall transit a third-party API. Consultant shall demonstrate this with the network cable unplugged.

2.3.3Status: available on request. Detail drawing pending.

Preliminary — not for construction

Detail D · FWD-D-D · Scale 1:1

Enablement

2.4.1Handover. Consultant shall document the system such that the Client's engineers can operate, extend and delete it without the Consultant present.

2.4.2Includes pairing sessions, runbooks, an evaluation playbook, and a written list of known limitations, ranked by how embarrassing they are.

2.4.3Vendor lock-in to Consultant shall be zero (0). Tolerance: none. Consultant remains on call thereafter; retainer optional.

Duration 1–2 wkCrew whoever built itDeliverable docs, training, on-call

3Sequence of operationsZone C

  1. 3.1

    Embed, week 0. Access, desks, laptops, Slack. Consultant sits with the people who do the work, and does some of it.

  2. 3.2

    Prototype, weeks 1–2. First working version against real Client data. The demo shall be a running system. A video of a running system is not a running system.

  1. 3.3

    Harden, weeks 3–8. Evaluation suite, permission model, audit logging, monitoring, fallbacks, human review. Failure modes shall be documented before the Client discovers them.

  2. 3.4

    Hand over, week 8+. Code, documentation, training. Consultant remains on call. Consultant shall not be a load-bearing member of the Client's architecture.

4General notesZone D

  1. 4.1

    All personnel supplied shall be senior engineers who write the code they talk about. No account managers, engagement leads or "AI strategists" shall be supplied, nor invoiced.

  2. 4.2

    Progress shall be measured in working software. Progress reports in other units (slides, dashboards of dashboards, "alignment") are not accepted.

  3. 4.3

    Consultant shall not produce slide decks unless explicitly requested in writing, and shall register a mild objection when so requested.

  4. 4.4

    Where AI is the wrong tool for a given workflow, Consultant shall state this in writing during Detail A, together with the correct tool. This clause has been invoked before and will be again.

  5. 4.5

    All deliverables shall be handed over: source, infrastructure definitions, evaluation data, documentation. Consultant retains no keys, no secrets, and no leverage.

  6. 4.6

    Personal data shall be processed in accordance with GDPR. Data residency EU/EEA unless otherwise specified by Client. Prompts, retrieved documents and logs containing personal data shall be treated as personal data, because they are. Any action a system cannot undo shall pass a human first.

  7. 4.7

    Estimates are given in weeks. Estimates given in quarters shall be considered a warning sign, including when given by Consultant.

  8. 4.8

    Dimensions in weeks unless otherwise noted. Do not scale from drawing. Do not scale from hype either.

6Request for informationZone F

Submit RFI to

hello@forward.example

Describe the workflow, the systems it touches, and what "better" would mean in numbers. Attachments welcome. Slides tolerated, in this direction only.

  1. 6.1

    Requests for information shall be answered within one (1) working day by an engineer, not an autoresponder.

  2. 6.2

    The first conversation is free of charge and free of slides. Bring the person who actually does the work.

  3. 6.3

    Location: Sweden. Remote is acceptable. Embedded is preferred. See 1.2.

  4. 6.4

    Consultant shall respond honestly to the question "do we need this at all". See 4.4.
